How to read it
The forward pass fills in every intermediate value (blue). The backward pass starts with ∂L/∂L = 1 at the output and walks back: each gate multiplies the gradient arriving from above by its own local derivative, then hands the result to its inputs. A node used in two places adds up what arrives from both.
